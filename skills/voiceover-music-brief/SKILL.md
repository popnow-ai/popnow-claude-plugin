# Voice-over and music brief

Sound is half of a video. Planning it before generation tells you which shots need native audio, which need silence for narration, and how long each line can be.

## Voice-over

- Pace: about 2.5 words per second in English for a calm read, 3 for energetic. Mark each line with its start time and duration.
- Add direction in brackets: [warm, slower], [pause 0.5s], [smile]. Spell out numbers and abbreviations the way they should be spoken.
- Leave 0.3 to 0.5 seconds of air at cuts.
- Voice description for casting or TTS: gender presentation if relevant, age range, accent, energy, references described in words. Do not ask to imitate a specific real person's voice, and only clone a voice with that person's documented consent.

## Music

- Mood in 3 adjectives, genre, tempo in BPM, instrumentation.
- Structure mapped to the edit: intro, build, drop or peak, outro, with timings that match key shots.
- Ducking notes: where music drops under narration.
- Licensing: the user needs rights for any track; do not suggest using commercial songs without a license.

## Sound effects

A table: shot | sound | timing | native model audio or added in the edit.

## Output

1. VO script table: time | line | direction.
2. Music brief.
3. SFX table.
4. A list of shots that should be generated with audio off because narration or music covers them.
