# Ad hooks and angles

On short-form platforms the first 1 to 3 seconds decide whether anyone watches. The cheapest test is to keep the body of the ad the same and swap only the opening shot. This skill writes those openings.

## Inputs

Ask only for what is missing:

- Product or offer, and who it is for.
- The one result or feeling the viewer should get.
- Proof you can show: a demo, a before/after, a number, a review.
- Platform and length (default: 9:16, 15 to 30 seconds).
- Anything off-limits: claims the brand cannot make, competitors it must not name, regulated topics.

## Angles to draw from

Pick 5 to 8 that fit the product. Do not use all of them.

| Angle | Opens with |
|---|---|
| Problem first | The frustrating moment the product removes |
| Result first | The finished outcome, then rewind to how |
| Pattern interrupt | An unexpected visual that makes people stop scrolling |
| Demo | The product working within the first second |
| Number | A specific, true figure ("3 minutes", "half the price") |
| Objection | The doubt people have, answered on screen |
| Comparison | Old way vs new way, side by side |
| Social proof | A real review or reaction (only if the user supplies it) |
| Curiosity | A question or unfinished action that needs the next shot |

## Rules

- Every claim must be true and supportable by the user. Do not invent reviews, statistics, awards or testimonials. Mark anything that needs proof with "[needs source]".
- No fake urgency, fake scarcity or misleading before/after shots.
- Keep on-screen text to 3 to 7 words; it must read in under 2 seconds.
- Write hooks that work with sound off.

## Output format

For each hook:

```
H3 · Angle: Number
First 2 seconds (visual): ...
On-screen text: "..."
Voice-over / line (optional): "..."
Shot prompt: <ready for the video-prompts format: shot, subject, action, setting, style, sound>
Why it might work: one sentence
```

Then list which hooks to test first (pick 3 that differ most) and what metric decides the winner (3-second view rate or hook rate first, then click-through).

Use the `video-prompts` skill to refine the shot prompts and `generation-budget` to price the test.
